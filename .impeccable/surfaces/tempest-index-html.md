# Surface brief: /tempest/

Mode: Operate. Visitor: the owner and anyone checking the station; reads live conditions, 24-hour trends, the 7-day forecast, yearly history. Constraints: no build step, WeatherFlow REST from the browser, labels and numbers only, DESIGN.md light and dark.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A station readout board, not a card grid. Live readings live in one sticky rail while a single stage swaps between 24 hours, 7 days and history; refuses the hero-metric plus 8 equal chart cards page.
OWN-WORLD: Federal Park Serigraph and Nocturne exactly as DESIGN.md: pine masthead band, framed sheet with print-strike shadow, ledger-striped rows, triple-stripe divider, Oswald numerals on framed signs, flat ink-colour series (ochre, pine, clay, mist, olive), no gradients, SVG glyphs instead of emoji.
STORY: The visitor sees the temperature and every current reading at once, picks a metric to study over the last 24 hours, scans the week as a ledger with hi/lo range bars, and opens a year when they want the long view.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Header and stripes, then the framed sheet. Masthead holds the Tempest Station title and the three view tabs. Left rail (about a third): Now sign with the large temperature, then the readings ledger with a wind compass. Right stage: large temperature chart with now/high/low, then a flush 4x2 grid of metric tiles that select the chart.
FORM: Index 7 of the grounded list (sticky Now rail plus tabbed stage), seed key 30ade2d0.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
